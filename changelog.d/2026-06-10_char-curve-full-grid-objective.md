### Characterization curve: full-wavelength-grid retune objective

The 5-sample broadband objective aliased the planet's chromatic phase
winding (>1 cycle across the J band beyond ~15-20 mas), so outer
characterization anchors collapsed ~50-1000x in contrast relative to
detection mode. The kilo_keck_6 characterization_curve stage now optimizes
on the full 20-bin grid: the smooth landscape lets 3 restarts find the good
basin (31 mas anchor: contrast 7.7e-8 vs 6.7e-5; 10 restarts at 5 samples
only reached 1.4e-7). Char mode now tracks and beats detection mode from
the IWA to near the OWA.
